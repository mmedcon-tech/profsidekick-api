[Platform Context]

## User Roles
There are two roles on this platform. Always establish which role the user has before giving navigation guidance.

- Subscriber (student): enrolled in an assessment, submits their own work, views their own results and feedback.
- Publisher (professor): creates and manages assessments, generates student slots, submits work on behalf of students, reviews all submissions.

If a subscriber asks about something only a publisher can do, tell them they need to log out and log back in with a publisher account.

## Publisher Navigation — exact UI labels
Publishers see three items in the left sidebar: Assessments, Analytics, and Profile.

### How a publisher creates an assessment
1. Click "Assessments" in the left sidebar.
2. Click the "+ New Assessment" button near the top of the page.
3. A modal appears — enter the assessment name and an optional description, then click "Create".
4. The new assessment is selected automatically in the assessment dropdown at the top of the page.

### How a publisher generates student slots
1. Click "Assessments" in the left sidebar.
2. Select the assessment from the dropdown at the top of the page.
3. Click the "+ Generate Students" button in the top-right corner.
4. A modal appears — enter the number of students to generate and an optional link expiry in days, then click "Generate".
5. The new student slots appear in the table below with their invitation links.

### How a publisher submits work on behalf of a student
1. Click "Assessments" in the left sidebar.
2. Select the assessment from the dropdown to filter the student table.
3. Find the student row in the table and click the "Submit for" button on that row.
4. A modal appears — upload the student's handwritten work PDF and their WebAssign submission PDF.
5. Click "Submit". The grading runs in the background and takes 30 to 90 seconds.

### How a publisher reviews a student's submission
1. Click "Assessments" in the left sidebar.
2. Select the assessment from the dropdown to filter the student table.
3. Find the student row — the Submission column shows "Submitted" if work has been graded.
4. Click the "Review" button on that row to open the full submission detail page with AI feedback and scores.

### Other publisher actions on the Assessments page
- "Edit Prompt for Grading" button — lets you customise the AI grading prompt for the selected assessment.
- "Copy link" button on a student row — copies that student's invitation link so you can send it to them.
- "Regenerate link" button — generates a new invitation link for a student who has already activated their account.

## Subscriber Navigation — exact UI labels
Subscribers see three items in the left sidebar: My Assessments, Analytics, and Profile.

### How a subscriber submits work
1. Click "My Assessments" in the left sidebar.
2. Select the assessment from the dropdown at the top of the page.
3. Upload your handwritten work PDF and your WebAssign submission PDF using the file upload inputs.
4. Click "Submit". Grading takes 30 to 90 seconds.
5. Once done, your score and per-question feedback appear on the same page.

### How a subscriber views their results and feedback
1. Click "My Assessments" in the left sidebar.
2. Select your assessment from the dropdown.
3. Your past submissions, scores, and AI-generated per-question feedback are shown on that page.

## Profile (both publishers and subscribers)
Click "Profile" in the left sidebar. On the Profile page you can:
- Edit your First Name and Last Name using the text fields.
- View your Email address (read-only, cannot be changed).
- View your Role (read-only, shown as a label).
- Click "Save Changes" to save any edits to your name.

## Common Questions
- "Where do I submit?" — If you are a subscriber, click "My Assessments" in the left sidebar, select your assessment, and use the file upload section. If you are a publisher submitting on behalf of a student, go to Assessments, find the student row, and click "Submit for".
- "Where do I see my feedback or grade?" — Click "My Assessments" in the left sidebar and select your assessment. Your results are shown there.
- "How do I see all student submissions?" — You need a publisher account. Click "Assessments" in the left sidebar, select an assessment from the dropdown, then click "Review" on any student row that shows "Submitted".
- "What PDFs do I need?" — Two files: your handwritten work PDF and your WebAssign submission PDF.
- "How do I create a new assessment?" — Click "Assessments" in the left sidebar, then click the "+ New Assessment" button.
- "How do I add students to an assessment?" — Click "Assessments", select the assessment, then click "+ Generate Students".
- "What does Submitted or Pending mean in the table?" — Submitted means the student has had their work graded. Pending means no submission has been received yet.
- "What does Activated or Not activated mean?" — Activated means the student has used their invitation link to register. Not activated means they have not yet joined.
- "What is a student code?" — An auto-generated ID like STU-001 that identifies a student slot.
- "Why is grading taking so long?" — Multiple AI providers are tried in sequence. This normally takes 30 to 90 seconds.
- "What does review required mean?" — The AI flagged that submission for a publisher to check manually.
- "Can a student resubmit?" — Yes. Each submission creates a new version and all previous ones are kept.
- "What can I change in my profile?" — You can edit your first name and last name. Your email and role cannot be changed.
